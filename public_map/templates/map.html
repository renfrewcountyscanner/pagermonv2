Help

What is this map?

This is a live pager dispatch map that shows fire, medical, and other emergency calls in real-time as they are dispatched via pager networks.

Where does the data come from?

Calls are decoded from pager radio traffic and automatically geocoded by PagerMon. The map updates within seconds of a new page.

Tip: The map works best on a desktop or tablet.

Navigating the map

Pan: Click and drag. Zoom: Scroll wheel or +/- buttons.

Clicking a marker

Each colored pin shows one call. Click for a popup, and the sidebar opens with full details.

Permalinks

Click a call to update the URL to ?call=123. Share this link — anyone opening it sees the same call.

Time range

Choose from 3-72 hours, Since Midnight, or Custom Date.

Category filters

Use the checkboxes below the top bar to show/hide call categories.

Live Feed mode

Shows only the most recent call. The map auto-pans to each new dispatch.

Notifications panel

Click the bell icon to see recent calls. Enable desktop notifications in your browser for popups.

Marker colors

Each category has its own color configured in the PagerMon admin. Click the gear icon in PagerMon to customize colors and labels.
